# Pricing Philosophy

**Status:** Approved Direction  
**Related:** [Commercial-Blueprint.md](./Commercial-Blueprint.md)

---

## Principles

1. **Value stages, not feature walls** — price maps to operational maturity.  
2. **Room-aware minimums** — room count can set a *minimum* package for fairness; upgrades for capability remain allowed.  
3. **Staff seats as capacity** — seat limits reinforce package boundaries and cost of service.  
4. **Multi-establishment is a business model, not a slider** — Business package.  
5. **Enterprise is organisational fit** — custom pricing, SLAs, security, integrations.  
6. **Transparent currency** — primary product UI uses ZAR for the home market.  
7. **Annual discount** — encourage commitment without locking customers out of monthly.  

## Entitlements

Support trials, complimentary plans, percentage/fixed discounts, and promo codes without forking the feature matrix. See [ARCHITECTURE/Entitlement-System.md](../ARCHITECTURE/Entitlement-System.md).

## What we do not do

- Surprise mid-cycle feature removal without communication  
- USD/ZAR mixed messaging in the same funnel  
- Skipping tiers in upsell recommendations by default
