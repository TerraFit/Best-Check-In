# Product Vision

**Status:** Approved Direction  
**Related:** [Product-Specification.md](./Product-Specification.md) · [Commercial-Blueprint.md](./Commercial-Blueprint.md)

---

## Vision statement

FastCheckIn is the **complete operational platform for independent hospitality businesses**—from statutory digital check-in through daily operations, staff, analytics, multi-establishment control, and enterprise-grade integration.

It is no longer only a digital guest registration app. It grows with the business: simple enough for a five-room guest house, structured enough for groups and enterprise operators.

---

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

- Guest houses, B&Bs, farm stays  
- Lodges and safari lodges  
- Boutique hotels and self-catering establishments  
- Small hotel groups and hospitality groups  

**Primary market (Current Implementation):** South Africa (POPIA, ZAR pricing, local payment rails).

---

## Outcomes we optimise for

1. **Legal compliance** — statutory-oriented guest data, signatures, consent, auditability  
2. **Time saved** — less paper, less re-keying, clearer staff workflows  
3. **Better decisions** — progressive analytics and AI maturity by package  
4. **Scalable operations** — from single property to multi-establishment  

---

## Non-goals (near term)

- Replacing large-chain PMS as the primary product for 200+ room hotels  
- Hiding all premium capability until payment (soft-lock preferred)  
- Competing primarily as a global channel manager  

---

## Principles (summary)

Simplicity · Automation · Operational efficiency · Guest experience · Legal compliance · Business intelligence · Scalability · Consistency
