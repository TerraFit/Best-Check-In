# Customer Personas

**Status:** Approved Direction

---

## 1. Owner-operator (Starter → Growth)

- Runs a 3–8 room guest house or B&B  
- Often on-site; limited admin time  
- Needs: paper replacement, compliance, simple reports  
- Pain: spreadsheets, lost forms, staff training on paper processes  

## 2. Operations manager (Growth → Pro)

- 8–15 rooms; small team (reception + housekeeping)  
- Needs: staff accounts, auditability, regional insight, lost & found  
- Pain: fragmented WhatsApp ops, no single source of guest truth  

## 3. Multi-property entrepreneur (Business)

- Owns 2+ related properties (lodge + cottages, small group)  
- Needs: one login mental model, roll-up analytics, consistent brand  
- Pain: multiple tools or duplicate logins  

## 4. Group / enterprise buyer (Enterprise)

- Brand standards, IT involvement, integrations  
- Needs: API, white label, SSO roadmap, custom reporting, contracts  
- Pain: consumer SaaS that cannot fit security or systems landscape  

## 5. Front-desk staff (all packages)

- Mobile-first; needs fast check-in and clear errors  
- Rarely sees billing; must never be blocked by unclear upgrade walls mid-guest  

---

Design and package messaging should speak to the **buyer** (1–4) while UX for check-in optimises for **persona 5**.
