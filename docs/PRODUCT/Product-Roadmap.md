- Single plan/feature configuration (SSOT)

- Harden live country analytics  

## Phase 4 — Pro professionalisation

- Lost & Found MVP  
- Business recommendation AI  
- Regional analytics reliability  

## Phase 5 — Business multi-establishment

- Establishments data model  
- Property switcher & roll-up  
- Multi-site analytics  

## Phase 6 — Enterprise readiness

- API, white-label approach, SSO roadmap  
- Custom reporting contracts
