01 / The business case

Paper still costs more than you think.

Traditional registration books create friction at arrival and trap useful operational and commercial information in physical pages.

Less manual administration

Reduce repeated data entry and paper handling so front-of-house teams can spend more time welcoming guests.

Know your guests

Guest origin, travel patterns, preferences and stay history become easier to retrieve and analyse.

See your performance

Clearer information helps management investigate room utilisation, occupancy patterns and market opportunities.

FastCheckIn perspective: Digital check-in is the entry point. The broader value is making guest information easier to access and use for service, market analysis and management decisions.
02 / Why FastCheckIn

One platform connecting guest experience with commercial performance.

Serve

Digital check-in on a property tablet or guest mobile via QR/link, ID capture, digital signatures and indemnities, returning-guest convenience and relevant guest preferences.

Understand

Explore guest origins from world to continent, country, region and city. Review guest profiles, stay history and market patterns from recorded information.

Grow

Business Snapshot reporting brings occupancy, room-night performance and unit-level utilisation together to inform pricing, promotion and packaging decisions.

FastCheckIn provides information to support decisions; it does not guarantee higher revenue or automatically make management decisions.
